What the service does

Agents Atelier lets you describe a website and have AI agents build its front end in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. You can review, change and download the result.

Your account

  • You must give a real email address and keep your password private.
  • You are responsible for what happens in your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your profile.
  • If you invite people to a project, they can see it (viewers) or change it and run its agents on your Claude account (editors). You can remove them at any time.
  • Paid plans are monthly subscriptions paid through PayPal. You can cancel at any time by moving to the Starter plan in your profile; the plan's features stop at once and nothing more is charged.

Your content

What you write, upload and build stays yours. You give us only the rights we need to store it, show it to you and process it to build your site.

Don't use the service for anything illegal, to copy other people's work without permission, to impersonate a real person or company, or to build pages meant to deceive or collect personal data unlawfully.

AI-generated results

The agents can make mistakes. Check the text, the code and any facts before you publish a site. The result is provided as it is, without a guarantee that it fits a particular purpose.

Availability and changes

We may change, pause or stop the service, and update these terms. When the changes are significant, we'll tell you before they apply.

Ending your use

We may suspend accounts that break these terms. You can download your data and delete your account from your profile at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, a hash of your password (never the password itself), your role, and the date you accepted these terms.
  • Optional answers you give: company, what describes you, what you build sites for and how you heard about us.
  • Your settings: language and theme, and your brand kit if you make one.
  • Your work: projects, prompts, uploaded images, the generated files, the chat with the agents, saved versions, and the time and estimated cost of each agent run.
  • Login sessions: a random token in a cookie, and its expiry date.
  • Projects you share: the people you invite (their email address and role), and your share links with how often they were opened.
  • Comments left through a share link: the name the visitor typed, their comment and where on the page they placed it. Visitors don't need an account and no cookie is set for them.
  • Payments: your plan, the PayPal subscription id and the payments PayPal reports (amount, date). We never see your card or PayPal login.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep you logged in.
  • To build and store your websites.
  • To send account emails: confirmation codes, password resets and security notices. We don't send marketing emails.
  • To protect the service, for example by limiting sign-ups and logins from one network.
  • To understand how the service is used, in totals only (sign-ups, builds and costs).

Who else processes it

  • Anthropic (Claude): your prompts, project details, brand kit and project files are sent to Claude so the agents can work on them.
  • PayPal: when you pay for a plan, PayPal handles the payment and receives the details you give it there.
  • Google Fonts: the pages load fonts from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • The email provider configured for this service delivers account emails.

We don't sell your data and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Cookies and storage

We use one cookie, which keeps you logged in. Your browser also stores your theme, language and a few display choices; this stays on your device.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, private agents, teams, brand kit, projects and their files. Expired login sessions and codes are removed or stop working.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: download all your data from your profile.
  • Correction: change your details on your profile.
  • Deletion: delete your account from your profile.
  • For anything else, including objections or complaints, write to the contact above. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
